June 2026 · Compliance · The Lead Story

Section 508, in practice: what an audit actually looks like from the inside

Most organizations meet Section 508 the hard way: a procurement checklist, a deadline three weeks out, and a 90-page PDF that defeats every screen reader on earth. The failures we find are almost boring. They are missing form labels, a focus trap in the “helpful” newsletter modal, contrast that lands at 4.3 when it needed 4.5, and documents exported with the accessibility tags stripped out.

Our cycle runs in three passes: an automated scan to clear the rubble, a manual pass with the mouse unplugged plus NVDA and VoiceOver, and a remediation report ranked for the developer who has to fix it on a Tuesday. The latest cycle closed with every critical finding fixed and re-tested, each one re-run until the result came back clean.
