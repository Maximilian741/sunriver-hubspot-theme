02 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

Why we quote custom, and why it’s fixed

A rate card tells you what an hour costs. It doesn’t tell you what your project costs, and that’s the only number that matters. So we scope the work first, then commit to a fixed fee.

  • Fixed-fee scoping means the price is set before we begin, no end-of-month surprises
  • We invest in understanding your work up front, so the estimate reflects your project and not a generic average
  • You can budget with confidence and compare apples to apples, because the number won’t drift mid-engagement
  • Senior-only staffing means the people scoping the work are the people doing it

{# ===== FAQ ===== #}
03 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you ask for a number

Why is pricing custom instead of a published rate?
Because no two projects are the same, and a single rate would either overcharge the simple work or undersell the hard work. We scope what you actually need, then quote a fixed fee for it, so the price reflects your project, not an average of everyone else’s.
Do you bill hourly?
No. We work to a fixed fee against an agreed scope. That keeps the incentive on shipping the outcome rather than running up hours, and it means you can plan your budget without watching a meter.
Can we start small?
Absolutely, that’s what Discovery is for. A short, scoped sprint gives you a real plan and a fixed price for the larger build before you commit to it. Plenty of clients start there and decide their next step with eyes open.
What’s included in a retainer?
A retainer reserves a standing block of senior engineering capacity each month, with priority response and a team that already knows your systems. It’s built for ongoing work and evolving roadmaps, where having senior hands on call matters more than a one-off project scope. We’ll tailor the size to your needs.
