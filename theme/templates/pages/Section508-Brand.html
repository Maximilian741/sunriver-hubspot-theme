What we focus on

We audit against the Revised Section 508 Standards (36 CFR Part 1194), which incorporate WCAG 2.0 Level AA by reference as the baseline for web and electronic content, plus the functional performance criteria and hardware provisions of the ICT standards. Coverage spans web interfaces, software and authoring tools, and any hardware or self-contained device controls in scope. Results are organized by the standard’s chapters so an agency reviewer can trace conformance clause by clause.
Documents are remediated to PDF/UA and the Section 508 document requirements, correct tag structure and reading order, alternative text, heading hierarchy, table header associations, list semantics, language metadata, and accessible form fields. We validate with the PAC (PDF Accessibility Checker), Adobe Acrobat Pro’s checker, and manual screen-reader review, since automated checks alone do not confirm logical reading order. You receive remediated source and output files plus a checklist evidencing each requirement met.
We deliver an Accessibility Conformance Report built on the current ITI VPAT 2.5 template, Section 508 edition, with per-criterion ratings of Supports, Partially Supports, Does Not Support, or Not Applicable and substantive remarks justifying each call. The ACR covers the WCAG 2.0 AA tables, the functional performance criteria, hardware, software, and support-documentation chapters as applicable. It is written to give a contracting officer defensible, evidence-backed conformance language for the procurement file.
We help vendors and agencies interpret, draft, and defend 508 language across the acquisition lifecycle, market research, Section 508 solicitation clauses (FAR 39.2), evaluation of submitted ACRs, and acceptance criteria for deliverables. For agencies we assess whether supplier conformance claims are credible; for vendors we prepare responses that map products honestly to the standards. The aim is to reduce procurement risk and avoid accessibility findings surfacing after award.
Remediation is delivered as a prioritized plan with code-level guidance, specific HTML, ARIA, and document-tagging changes tied to each failed provision, sequenced so the highest-impact barriers are resolved first. We support developers through implementation and then re-test the fixes so the conformance claim in the ACR is backed by verified evidence rather than intent. A final attestation records what was retested and confirmed, keeping the claim defensible under audit.
