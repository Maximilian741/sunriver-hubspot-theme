{# ===== FAQ ===== #}
05 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask

The questions teams ask us most when planning a new site or a rebuild.

Which platform should I be on?
It comes down to who edits the site and how much custom behavior you need. If marketing owns the content and you want CRM integration, HubSpot CMS is hard to beat. If you need full control or peak performance, a custom Next.js, Astro, or Eleventy build wins. WordPress and Squarespace each have their place too. We’ll recommend based on your team and goals, not a platform we’re partial to.
Can you migrate my existing site?
Yes. We move content, preserve your URLs with proper redirects so you don’t lose search rankings, and carry over, or rebuild, the pieces worth keeping. Migrations are planned so the cutover is boring: the new site goes live, the old links still work, and traffic keeps flowing.
How do you make sites fast?
We set performance budgets up front and build to them, lean markup, optimized images, minimal JavaScript, and fast delivery. Then we measure against Core Web Vitals rather than trusting a single lab score. Speed is a feature, and it’s easier to protect when it’s a requirement from day one instead of a cleanup task at the end.
Who maintains it after launch?
Your call. We build sites your own team can run, with documentation and a walkthrough, so you’re never dependent on us. If you’d rather we stay on for updates, security patches, and improvements, we’re happy to. Either way, you own everything.
