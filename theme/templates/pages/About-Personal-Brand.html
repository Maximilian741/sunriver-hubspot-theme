Why I do this

The honest version.

Helena started with a last-chance gold strike in 1864, and my family has worked this valley ever since. Five generations of Casteels read the ground for a living. Mining teaches a few things that stick: assay the rock before you sell it, respect the people working beside you, and only promise a vein you have actually seen.

I started SunRiver to build the version I would want as the client. A big agency quotes thirty grand, puts a junior on it, bills you for the learning curve, and keeps the keys to your own accounts when it is done. So instead you get one senior person who scopes the work honestly, names a fixed fee before we start, and hands you everything in your name when we finish. Your domain, your hosting, your code, your logins. A few hundred a year in costs you control, and everything held in your own hands.

That is the whole pitch. Senior work, a fair number, and a person who picks up the phone. The cats came with the deal, and they work for treats.

Max, Founder
