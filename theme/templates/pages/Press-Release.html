SunRiver Consulting today announced the HIPAA Cloud Accelerator, a packaged 12-week engagement that compresses HIPAA-aligned cloud migrations for mid-market health systems. Three pilot clients shipped in Q1, with a fourth set to launch in early Q3.

Why now
