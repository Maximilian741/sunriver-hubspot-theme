What we focus on

We configure Incident, Problem, and Change management aligned to ITIL practice and the way your team actually responds, with assignment groups, categorization, and clear states from logging through resolution. Change is set up with risk assessment, approval policies, and change types — standard, normal, emergency — so the right rigor applies to the right change. The process fits how the team works, which is what drives adoption rather than resistance.
We populate and maintain the CMDB through Discovery and Service Mapping so configuration items and their relationships reflect reality, not a stale spreadsheet, and we apply the CSDM framework so the data is structured correctly. Identification and reconciliation rules prevent duplicate CIs from polluting the database. An accurate CMDB is what makes impact analysis, change risk, and service mapping actually trustworthy.
We build automation in Flow Designer for approvals, assignment, and SLA tracking so work routes and escalates without manual nudging, and configure SLA definitions with the right conditions and pause rules so the clock reflects reality. Notifications and tasks are generated automatically at the right points. The aim is a system that moves work forward on its own rather than waiting on someone to remember.
We connect ServiceNow to monitoring, HR, and directory systems through Integration Hub spokes and REST so records stay in sync — events create incidents, HR data drives onboarding, and directory data keeps users and groups current. Integrations are built with error handling and logging so failures are visible rather than silent. This keeps the platform a single source of truth instead of one more island of data.
We design Service Catalog items with record producers, variable sets, and request workflows that give people a clear, self-service front door to IT, with multi-stage fulfillment routing each request to the right team. Catalog items are scoped and governed so the catalog stays curated rather than sprawling. A well-built catalog deflects routine tickets and gives requesters a predictable, trackable experience.
