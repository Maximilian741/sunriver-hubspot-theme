{# ===== FAQ ===== #}
05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you book an audit

How long does an audit take?
It depends on scope, but most focused engagements run one to three weeks from kickoff to report. We size the timeline during scoping and tell you the number before any work starts.
Which standards do you test against?
For security, the OWASP Top 10 and STRIDE threat modeling. For accessibility, WCAG 2.0 through 2.2 at A and AA, plus Section 508 for federal-facing work. We can also produce VPAT-ready documentation and support SOC 2 readiness.
Do you help with remediation, or just hand over findings?
We help. Every finding ships with reproduction steps and remediation guidance, and we pair with your engineers during fixes, then re-test to confirm closure. The goal is issues resolved, not just reported.
How is pricing structured?
Fixed-fee, scoped up front. Once we understand the targets and standards, you get a single number for the engagement, no hourly meter, no surprise change orders.
