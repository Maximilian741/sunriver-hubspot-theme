What we focus on

We configure Entra ID with Conditional Access policies that evaluate user, device, location, and risk before granting access, enforce MFA, and move toward phishing-resistant methods where appropriate. Legacy authentication protocols are blocked, and we apply named locations and sign-in risk policies so the right people get in and attackers do not. We document each policy and its exclusions so the access model is auditable rather than a tangle of one-off rules.
We configure Exchange Online mail flow, connectors, and anti-spam and anti-phishing through Defender for Office 365, including safe links and safe attachments, so mail is reliable and protected. Teams is governed with naming policies, expiration, guest-access controls, and sensible defaults so collaboration does not sprawl into ungoverned sprawl of channels. The result is communication that works and is governed rather than chaotic.
We design SharePoint site architecture — hub sites, team and communication sites, and a deliberate permission model — so documents stay findable and access is scoped to who genuinely needs it. We avoid the broken-inheritance permission mess by using groups and sensitivity labels rather than per-item grants. Search and metadata are set up so people find documents instead of emailing around for them.
We enroll devices into Intune with compliance policies covering encryption, OS version, and security posture, and use configuration profiles and app protection to keep company data protected on managed endpoints. Conditional Access ties access to device compliance, so an out-of-policy device cannot reach corporate data. We support both corporate-owned and BYOD scenarios with policies appropriate to each.
We apply Microsoft security baselines and Defender policies tenant-wide, align settings against Secure Score and CIS benchmarks, and turn on the auditing needed to prove the configuration. Where it fits your licensing, we layer in Purview retention and sensitivity labeling for data governance. You get a hardened tenant with an audit trail that stands up to a security or compliance review.
