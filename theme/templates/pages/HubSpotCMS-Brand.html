What we focus on

We build custom themes and drag-and-drop modules in HubL with thoughtfully designed module fields, grouping and repeaters, so editors compose pages inside guardrails that keep them on-brand. Theme-level style fields expose colors, spacing, and typography as controlled options rather than free-form CSS, which means the design system holds up after launch. Editors get real flexibility within boundaries, not a blank canvas that drifts away from the brand.
We write HubSpot serverless functions for the logic templates cannot express on their own, custom form handling, third-party API lookups, gated submissions, and data enrichment at request time. Functions are kept secure with secrets stored in HubSpot’s environment rather than hardcoded, and rate and payload limits are respected. This is how we add genuine application behavior to a CMS site without bolting on a separate backend.
We configure HubSpot memberships so gated content and members-only pages are tied to CRM contact lists, giving you registration, login, and access control without a separate portal product. Access is driven by list membership so marketing and sales can manage who sees what using the CRM they already run. It is well suited to customer portals, partner resources, and premium content behind a login.
We ship clean, semantic markup with structured data (schema.org/JSON-LD), proper canonical and meta handling, and a deliberate heading hierarchy so search engines can parse the page. We measure and tune Core Web Vitals, image sizing and lazy-loading, deferred scripts, and minimized render-blocking assets, so pages load fast on real devices. The aim is technical SEO that holds up under audit and pages that rank and perform rather than just look finished.
