What we focus on

Running open models locally with Ollama means your prompts and documents never leave hardware you control — no third party ever sees the data. We deploy capable open-weight models (Llama, Mistral, Qwen, and similar) sized to your machines, from a workstation to a GPU server, and wire them into the same kinds of assistants and automations we build on hosted APIs. For confidential, regulated, or air-gapped work, this is often the only acceptable architecture, and modern open models are genuinely good enough for most everyday tasks.
When a hosted API is the right tool for the quality you need, the default consumer terms aren’t the only option. We configure enterprise and API-tier access from Anthropic and OpenAI for zero data retention and no training on your inputs, and we document the exact setting and the provider’s data-handling commitment so the guarantee is verifiable rather than assumed. We make the distinction explicit: the free consumer chatbot and a properly configured API account have very different data postures, and we set up the latter.
We treat personal data as something to minimize before it ever reaches a model. Depending on the workflow, that means redacting or masking PII at the boundary — names, account numbers, health or financial details — sending only what the task genuinely requires, and keeping identifiers on your side where they belong. We document what is transmitted and what is withheld so you have a clear, defensible record of how personal data flows through the system, which is exactly what a privacy review or breach inquiry will ask for.
We design with your compliance obligations in view — HIPAA, FERPA, attorney-client confidentiality, and similar regimes — rather than bolting controls on afterward. That covers where data physically lives, who and what can access the model and logs, encryption in transit and at rest, and an audit trail of usage. We’re not your lawyers and we’ll say so, but we build the architecture to support your requirements and document the design decisions so your compliance team and auditors can review the reasoning, not just the result.
Privacy and cost both improve when the model is right-sized to the job. A small local model handles the routine, high-volume cases entirely on your infrastructure — private by construction and free of per-call fees — while you reserve a hosted frontier model for the genuinely hard tasks that need its reasoning. We’ll often design a hybrid: sensitive or bulk work stays local, and only carefully scrubbed, non-sensitive requests go to an external API, giving you strong privacy without giving up capability where it counts.
