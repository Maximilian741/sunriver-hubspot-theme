What we focus on

We hand-build CMS Hub themes and modules in HubL with properly typed fields — text, image, choice, repeaters, and group fields — plus drag-and-drop areas and flexible columns, so marketers compose and reorder full pages without a developer. Modules are scoped with their own CSS and JS and reused across templates, which keeps the design consistent and the codebase DRY. Editors get freedom inside guardrails, not a blank canvas they can break.
We structure the whole site for maintainability: a coherent set of templates, global header/footer/content modules edited once and reflected everywhere, and theme settings that expose brand colors, fonts, and spacing as controls instead of hard-coded values. That governance means a color or logo change propagates site-wide in a click, and new pages inherit the system automatically. Your team maintains a consistent site in-house long after handoff.
When standard modules hit their ceiling we write HubSpot serverless functions in Node.js for custom form logic, third-party API lookups, gated content, and integrations the platform does not offer natively. These run inside HubSpot’s infrastructure with secrets stored securely, so there is no separate server for your team to babysit. We use them deliberately for the gaps, keeping the bulk of the site on fast, cacheable native rendering.
We drive dynamic content from HubDB tables and CRM objects so pages like location finders, resource libraries, and team directories render from data you already maintain — update a row and the page updates itself. Combined with smart content and CRM-based personalization, returning visitors and known contacts see content tailored to their lifecycle stage. It scales to hundreds of pages without anyone hand-building each one.
We wire SEO into the build rather than bolting it on: clean canonical and meta structure, an organized topic-cluster and pillar architecture, and managed 301 redirects so no equity is lost in migration. Everything connects to HubSpot’s native SEO recommendations and analytics, so rankings and crawl issues are tracked and actioned in the same place your marketers already work. Search visibility becomes measured, not guessed.
