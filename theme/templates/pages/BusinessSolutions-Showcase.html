{# ===== FAQ ===== #}
04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you migrate or integrate

Which cloud should we be on?
We lead with AWS because it’s where we have the deepest hands-on depth, and its breadth fits most workloads we see. That said, the right answer depends on your existing licensing, team skills, and compliance needs, we’ll tell you honestly if you’re better served staying where you are.
How do you keep a migration from breaking things?
We migrate in small, reversible increments rather than one big cutover. Each workload moves with a tested rollback path, parallel-run validation where it matters, and clear go/no-go checkpoints, so a problem means we step back, not scramble.
How do you actually control AWS cost?
FinOps is part of the design. We right-size compute, tier storage, apply tagging and budgets, evaluate Savings Plans and Reserved capacity, and set anomaly alerts, so the bill is explainable and you stop paying for idle capacity.
Will you support our existing stack, or rip and replace?
We support what works. If your HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft 365, or ServiceNow setup is sound, we integrate and tune it rather than replace it. We only recommend a change when the current state is costing you more than the move would.
