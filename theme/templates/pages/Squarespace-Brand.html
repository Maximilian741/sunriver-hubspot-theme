What we focus on

We start from a strong Squarespace 7.1 template and push it well past the defaults with custom CSS and targeted code injection, refined typography, spacing, custom section layouts, and interactions the stock template cannot produce. The result reads as designed specifically for your brand while keeping all the hosting, security, and reliability Squarespace handles for you. You get a distinctive site without the maintenance burden of a fully custom stack.
We set up Squarespace Commerce end to end: product and variant catalog, a clean checkout, shipping rates by zone or weight, and tax configured for where you actually sell, plus payment processing through Stripe or PayPal. Inventory, order notifications, and abandoned-cart recovery are configured so operations work from day one. You can take real orders the moment the site goes live, not weeks later.
We migrate pages, posts, images, and product data into Squarespace and, critically, map every old URL to its new home with 301 redirects so existing search rankings and inbound links carry over intact. We preserve metadata and image alt text during the move and verify there are no broken links or orphaned pages afterward. The launch is a clean cutover, not a ranking reset.
We give you a guided walkthrough plus a short written guide built around your actual site, how to edit pages, add blog posts, manage products, and swap images, not a generic Squarespace manual. The goal is that routine content edits become something your team does confidently in minutes, so you are not paying us for every small change. Editing your own site stops being intimidating.
Squarespace templates are not accessible out of the box, so we audit and fix the gaps: color contrast brought to WCAG AA, a correct heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text, and keyboard-navigable menus and forms. We check the polished design against a screen reader and keyboard-only use, then remediate what fails. The site looks good and is genuinely usable for visitors with disabilities, which also reduces legal exposure.
