How we work

Six principles we put in writing.

Fixed-fee

A miner assays the ore before chasing it. We scope the work first, then give you one written number. The figure you agree to is the figure you pay.

Accessible & secure

WCAG 2.2 and Section 508 are part of the build from the first line. Security gets the same treatment, planned in and tested throughout.

Yours to keep
