{# ===== FAQ ===== #}
05 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask

The questions we hear most from teams scoping their first — or next — app.

How long does it take to build an app?
It depends on scope, but most focused apps move from kickoff to a usable first release in a matter of weeks, not quarters. We build in increments so you’re seeing — and steering — real software early rather than waiting for one big reveal at the end.
Native or cross-platform — which should I choose?
If your app leans hard on the camera, sensors, or platform-specific polish, native (Kotlin, Swift) usually wins. If you need to reach iOS and Android on a tighter budget and the experience is mostly screens and data, React Native or a shared web core can get you there for less. We’ll recommend honestly based on your users and budget — not on what keeps us busy.
Can you handle App Store and Play Store submission?
Yes. We prepare builds, listings, privacy disclosures, and review materials, and we shepherd the app through Apple’s and Google’s review processes. If a reviewer pushes back, we handle the response so you’re not deciphering rejection notices on your own.
What happens after launch?
Apps aren’t “done” at launch — operating systems update, devices change, and your needs grow. We offer ongoing support to monitor crashes, ship updates, and keep the app current with each OS release. You’ll always be talking to the senior engineers who built it.
