What we focus on

We build deal and ticket pipelines whose stages mirror how your team actually moves work, with entry and exit criteria defined for each stage so the funnel reflects reality rather than a generic default. Required properties at each stage keep records complete, and stage automation handles the busywork of moving and updating deals. The payoff is forecasting and reporting that leadership can actually believe.
We design workflows that route leads, assign owners by territory or round-robin, trigger follow-up tasks, and escalate when an SLA is at risk, so work moves forward without sitting in someone’s inbox. Enrollment criteria and re-enrollment are set deliberately to avoid the loops and double-sends that plague hasty automation. We document each workflow so the team understands what fires, when, and why.
We deduplicate contacts and companies, standardize properties, and put validation and required-field rules in place so bad data is stopped at entry rather than cleaned up endlessly afterward. We reconcile formatting, fix association gaps, and establish a clear data governance approach for ongoing hygiene. Clean inputs are what make every downstream report and automation trustworthy.
We build dashboards and custom report types on properties that are reliable and consistently populated, so the metrics surfaced — pipeline velocity, conversion by stage, source attribution — reflect the business rather than artifacts of messy data. Reports are tailored to each audience, from rep-level activity to executive summary. Because the underlying data is clean, the numbers hold up to scrutiny.
We connect HubSpot to your finance, support, and marketing systems through native integrations, the HubSpot API, or middleware such as a sync platform, with field mappings and conflict rules defined so records stay consistent across tools. This makes the CRM the hub of record rather than one more disconnected silo. We monitor the sync and document the integration so it stays healthy as your stack changes.
